polso has four things to look at. Everything you send maps to one of them.

## Wings

The black notch grows two wings, `left` and `right`. Each one is a meter with its own
[indicator](../../customize/indicators/), unit and thresholds. Every value you send is appended to that wing's
history (30 samples by default) and colors it by health:

| health | when | color |
|---|---|---|
| `ok` | below `warn` | <span class="p-swatch" style="color:#30d158"></span>green |
| `warn` | at or above `warn` | <span class="p-swatch" style="color:#ffc70a"></span>yellow |
| `crit` | at or above `crit` | <span class="p-swatch" style="color:#ff453b"></span>red, the "now" dot breathes |
| `unknown` | no data yet, or stale | <span class="p-swatch" style="color:#7d7d82"></span>gray |

With `invert: true` low is bad (battery, throughput) and the comparisons flip. A sender can also force a health
or a text label per value. A wing that gets nothing for `staleAfter` seconds goes gray.

## Pings

A ping is a "something happened" with no number attached. A comet runs along the notch outline, starting from the
side of the targeted wing, and that wing glows twice. A ping without a `slot` runs across the whole notch and
glows both wings. Color it with `ok`/`warn`/`crit`/`info`, a name (`purple`) or `#rrggbb`.

## Alerts

Alerts drop a banner out of the notch with sparks and a rim glow tinted by severity (`info` blue, `warn` yellow,
`crit` red). They stay up `alert.duration` seconds (5 by default) with a countdown bar.

- Alerts arriving while one is showing **queue up** (`alert.queue: false` makes the new one replace it).
- The same `title` again **bumps a counter** (`×3`) instead of replaying.
- Hovering the banner opens the panel and dismisses it.

## The panel

Hover the notch and it expands into a panel: a card per wing with the big value, a sparkline with warn/crit guides
and the sample count, then a log of recent pings and alerts. Alerts with a `url` open it on click. The footer shows
config, socket and source errors, plus Settings and Quit.

## Under the hood

```
 scripts, cron, CI ──► ~/.config/polso/sock ──┐
                         (NDJSON, mode 600)   ├──► Store ──► notch (SwiftUI, NSPanel above the menu bar)
 sources: exec · prometheus · scrape ─────────┘
```

- One `Store` holds both wings, the ping counters, the alert queue and the log. Socket lines and source results
  go through the same parser, so a source can do anything a socket client can.
- The panel window floats above the menu bar on every Space. It ignores the mouse except while expanded.
- Animations run only while something moves. Idle CPU sits at ~0%.
- `~/.config/polso/config.json` is checked every 2s and on hover, so edits apply live.
